## 1. The Problem (45 sec)

Every single day, millions of Indians lose their things.

Keys. Wallets. Bags. Luggage. Vehicles. Pets.

It's not just an inconvenience — it costs us time, money, and peace of mind. A lost wallet means cancelled cards. A lost key means a locksmith at midnight. A lost delivery vehicle means thousands in operational downtime.

Now, the so-called solutions in the market?

**AirTags** cost ₹5,000+ and only work on iPhone. That excludes 95% of India.

**Samsung SmartTags** lock you into their ecosystem. Great if you have a Samsung phone. Useless if you don't.

**Cheap Bluetooth trackers** from Amazon at ₹500 work up to 10 meters. That's it. Ten meters. Useless the moment you step out of your house.

On the enterprise side, fleet tracking solutions cost ₹15,000–₹30,000 per vehicle. Most Indian small businesses can't afford that. So they just deal with the losses.

This is not a small problem.

India's GPS tracker market is **₹1,214 crores** and growing at **16% CAGR**.

And nobody is solving it for the Indian consumer or the Indian small business at a price they can afford.

## 2. The Solution — TagX (1 min)

TagX is India's first affordable, cross-platform, AI-powered tracking tag.

**A ₹2,699 tag** that works with any phone — iPhone or Android. Bluetooth for close-range finding. GPS + cellular for city-wide tracking. And a cloud platform that stores your location history, sends smart alerts, and gets smarter over time.

For consumers, it's peace of mind. You attach a TagX to your keys, your wallet, your bag, your pet's collar — and you always know where they are.

For families, we have the **Family Plan at ₹599–₹999 per year** — multi-device tracking, location sharing, and alerts for the whole household.

For power users, **AI Pro at ₹999–₹1,500 per year** — geofencing, AI-powered insights ("You left your bag at the office"), and smart notifications.

For enterprises, the fleet packs start at **₹4,999 per unit** with GPS + NB-IoT cellular connectivity, a real-time SaaS dashboard, geofence management, and API integration — at 60–70% lower cost than legacy solutions.

**What makes TagX different:**

One, it's **cross-platform**. Works on any phone. No ecosystem lock-in.

Two, it's **affordable**. ₹2,699 — less than half the price of an AirTag.

Three, it's **built for India**. Local support. Indian maps. Indian cellular networks. Designed for Indian conditions.

And four, it's a **platform, not a product**. The hardware is the entry point. The subscription is where the long-term value lives — for us and for the customer. Their data stays in our cloud. They stay with us for years.

## 3. The Business Model (1 min 30 sec)

TagX makes money three ways:

**Layer one — Hardware (60-70% of revenue).**

We sell tags at ₹3,000–₹4,500 for consumers. Fleet packs at ₹4,999–₹5,999 per unit for businesses. Enterprise Hubs at ₹14,999. Each tag costs us ₹830 to make in Year 1 — that's a 72% gross margin. By Year 5, our BOM drops to ₹660 as volumes scale, pushing gross margins past 90%.

**Layer two — Subscriptions (15-25% of revenue).**

Family Plan at ₹599–₹999 per year. AI Pro at ₹999–₹1,500 per year. Our attach rates start at 15% in Year 1 and grow to 40%+ by Year 5. People don't churn because their data is in our cloud.

Here's the unit economics: AI Pro costs us ₹100 per user per year for inference. That's a 90%+ margin.

**Layer three — B2B services (5-10% of revenue).**

SaaS fleet console at ₹499–₹999 per month. API access at ₹24,999 per year. Cellular data pass-through at ₹60–₹75 per device per month. White-label OEM deals.

By Year 5, B2B contributes ~₹1.5 crores to our top line with minimal COGS.

**Our go-to-market:**

Year 1 and 2, we sell direct — our website, Instagram and YouTube marketing, influencer unboxings. Our marketing budget is ₹1 lakh in Year 1. We move lean.

From Year 3, we hit Amazon India for B2C scale and build a dedicated B2B sales team.

By Year 5, we are spending ₹20 lakhs on marketing and reaching customers across every tier-1 and tier-2 Indian city.

## Appendix — Likely Panel Questions

**Q: AirTag is ₹5,000. How can you sell at ₹2,699?**
> China-sourced BOM. Our components (GPS module, BLE chip, battery) cost ₹750 in Y1 volumes, dropping to ₹600 by Y5. AirTag's BOM is estimated at ~$30 (₹2,500). We design for cost from day one. No NFC U1 chip, no precision finding hardware — features that add cost but most Indians won't use.

**Q: Why China and not Make in India?**
> China's EMS ecosystem is mature and cost-competitive for low-volume hardware. At our Y1 scale (250 units), Indian EMS isn't viable. At Y4+ scale (3,000+ units), we evaluate Indian partners under PLI schemes.

**Q: Subscription attach rates seem aggressive.**
> We start at 15% in Y1 (conservative) and grow to 40-45% by Y5. Comparable benchmarks: Tile's subscription attach rate is ~35%. Garmin's is ~50%. Our cloud data lock-in gives us retention.

**Q: What about stolen tags?**
> Privacy-first design. Tags don't store location history. Data lives in the cloud behind authentication. Users can remotely wipe a stolen tag. Privacy alerts notify you if an unknown TagX is travelling with you.

**Q: Why not bootstrap Year 1-2?**
> Tooling (₹10L) and BIS certification (₹5L) are hard upfront costs. You cannot defer certification — selling uncertified electronics in India is illegal. The ₹42L ask covers these non-negotiable investments.

**Q: Total addressable market seems small.**
> ₹1,214Cr GPS tracker market — that's just hardware. Add subscriptions, SaaS, cellular data, and the serviceable market is 3-4× larger. And with 16% CAGR, the 2030 market will be ₹2,500Cr+. We only need 0.6%.
